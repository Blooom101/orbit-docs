An [Orbie](/concepts/orbies) is a ready-to-run agent with its integrations pre-wired. Launching one is the fastest way to add a working teammate to your AI company — no setup, no prompt-engineering, no wiring.

## Steps

1. Open **Orbies** (provisioned agents) in your dashboard.
2. Browse the ready-made agents and pick one that matches the job you need done.
3. Give it your specifics — the goal, and anything it needs to know for your business.
4. **Launch.** It starts running in Orbit Cloud right away.

That's it. The integrations it needs (like [Apify](https://apify.com) for web data, and others) are already connected, so it can actually do the work — not just talk about it.

## What happens after launch

Your new Orbie behaves like any other member of your team:

- It appears on your [org chart](/use/organize-org-chart) — give it a role and department.
- It reads your [decisions](/use/update-decision) before it acts, so it stays on-policy.
- It files its work to your [Vault](/concepts/vault) and shows up in your [Briefing](/use/read-briefing).
- You can [message it](/use/send-message), [pause it](/use/pause-agent), or put it [on a schedule](/use/scheduled-agents).

## Orbie vs. connecting your own tool

Two ways to get an agent working — pick whichever's faster for the job:

| | Launch an Orbie | [Connect your own tool](/use/connect-claude) |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | One click, pre-wired | You connect Claude/Codex/etc. |
| Integrations | Already included | You add what you need |
| Best for | Getting a job done fast | Work tied to your own tools & files |

Both plug into the same brain, so you can run a mix.
