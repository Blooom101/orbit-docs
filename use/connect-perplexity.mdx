Perplexity can connect to Orbit through its custom remote connectors, so your research in Perplexity reads from and writes to the same Orbit brain as your other tools.

<Note>
Custom connectors require a paid Perplexity plan (**Pro, Max, or Enterprise**). They aren't available on the free tier.
</Note>

## Steps

1. In Perplexity, open **Settings → Connectors** (custom / remote connectors).
2. Add a new connector pointing at your Orbit MCP URL:
   ```
   https://app.orbitagents.com/api/public/mcp/[your-key]
   ```
3. Save and enable it.

## What happens

Perplexity reads your active decisions and knowledge before a task, and files its answers and findings to your Orbit Vault — the same Vault your Claude, Codex, and other agents use. Cited research done in Perplexity becomes context everything else can build on.

## If the connection is rejected

Some Perplexity setups expect an OAuth discovery endpoint (`/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server`) during connector setup. If your connection is refused, that's the usual fix — reach out via [the community](/help/community) and we'll help get it wired up.
