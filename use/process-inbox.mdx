The [Inbox](/concepts/inbox) is your daily action queue — everything new your agents filed, waiting for your review.

## Steps

1. Open the **Inbox** from your dashboard.
2. Start with **Needs Action** — conflicts, flags, and anything needing a decision. Handle these first.
3. Move to **Ready to File** — standard outputs. Review each and approve in one click to send it to your [Vault](/concepts/vault).
4. Use **Recently Actioned** to undo anything you approved by mistake.

## A good daily rhythm

Read the [Briefing](/use/read-briefing) for the headlines, then work the Inbox top to bottom until it's empty. An empty Inbox means every piece of agent work has been reviewed.
