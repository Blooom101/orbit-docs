This is how your agents work your **inbox and outreach** — LinkedIn, Instagram, Gmail, and more — acting as *you*, on your own accounts. You link each account yourself; Orbit doesn't hold these credentials centrally.

## How it works

- **Bring your own account.** You connect *your* LinkedIn, Instagram, Gmail, etc. — Orbit acts through your login, not a shared bot.
- **$7/month per connected account.** Each account you link is a small add-on.
- **Connect in the dashboard:** Settings → Integrations → **Your accounts**.

## What you can connect

| Channel | Agents can… |
|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | list & read chats, send messages, start chats, send invites, post, comment |
| **Instagram** | read & answer DMs, **and publish** photo posts, carousels, reels, and stories |
| **Gmail** | list & read emails, send emails |
| **Outlook / IMAP** | list, read, send email |
| **WhatsApp, X (DMs), Telegram, Messenger** | read & send messages |
| **Twilio (SMS)** | send and read texts from your own number |

Read and list actions aren't rate-limited. **Sends** are capped per account — see [Messaging rate limits](/reference/messaging-limits). A LinkedIn account on Sales Navigator sends up to **50 invites a day** instead of 20.

<Note>
Connecting a Gmail or Outlook account also brings your **calendar** into Orbit, read-only and free, so agents can check your meetings before booking. See [the Calendar](/concepts/calendar).
</Note>

### Text your leads (Twilio SMS)

Connect your own Twilio number (Account SID, Auth Token, number) and agents send and read texts within safe limits (300 a day, 6 per person a day). Texts in land in the People [Inbox](/concepts/inbox) as SMS conversations, and replies from the inbox go out by SMS. A missed call can get a text back. STOP and START are honoured, and texted numbers become [CRM](/concepts/crm) people.

## The guardrails baked in

Every messaging tool is built to protect your accounts and your reputation. Agents are instructed to:

- **Personalise every message** — no copy-paste blasts. On LinkedIn, personalise every invite; reply in your voice, 2–4 sentences, no emoji walls; never pitch in the first message.
- **Answer inbound first.** On Instagram, only open new threads with people who engaged, and **never put a link in an opening DM.**
- **Keep email clean.** Always include a subject, quote what you're replying to, and hand anything legal, financial, or contentious back to you.
- **Never send cold sequences from your mailbox.** Gmail/Outlook/IMAP are for replies and triage only — use a dedicated cold-email tool for sequences.
- **Send only your exact text.** Nothing is sent except what's passed — no auto-invented content, and never made-up facts about the recipient.

## Who decides when something actually sends?

Important: **Orbit's send tools do exactly what they're told — the platform itself does not add an approval step.** Whether a message goes out automatically or waits for your sign-off is controlled by **your AI client and the agent's instructions**, not by Orbit:

- Run the agent with step-by-step approval (or a `needs_you → approve` gate) and you review before anything sends.
- Give it autonomy and it sends within the rate limits and guardrails above.

So decide your comfort level per agent: tighter approval for cold outreach, more autonomy for inbound replies. The [`needs_you`](/reference/mcp-tools#where-a-result-lands-the-kind-field) result kind is the clean way to route a draft to your Inbox for approval before it goes out.
