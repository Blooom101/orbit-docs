If you already have an account with a service, you can connect it to Orbit with your own API key ("bring your own key"). Your agents can then use it as part of their work, and you pay that service directly — Orbit just orchestrates.

## How to connect

1. Open **Settings → Integrations → Your tools** in your dashboard.
2. Pick the service you want to connect.
3. Paste your API key for that service.
4. Save. Your agents can now use it.

## What you can connect

| Service | What agents can do with it |
|---|---|
| **Notion** | Read/write your workspace docs & databases |
| **Airtable** | Read/write your bases |
| **HubSpot** | Work with your CRM records |
| **GitHub** | Work with your repos, issues, PRs |
| **Linear** | Work with issues and projects |
| **Slack** | Post and read in your channels |
| **Stripe** (read-only) | Read payments/customers data |
| **Resend** | Send transactional email |
| **Semrush** | Pull SEO / keyword data |
| **Firecrawl** | Scrape pages into clean markdown |
| **Perplexity** | Get cited answers from the web |
| **Instantly** | Run cold-email campaigns (add leads, list campaigns) |
| **PosteAhora** | Publish to social (Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, Threads, Facebook, Bluesky, YouTube, Pinterest) |

<Note>
This list grows over time. If a service you use isn't here yet, ask for it in [the community](/help/community).
</Note>

## Provisioned vs. BYOK — which do I use?

- **Need web data, scraping, buying signals, or images?** Those are [provisioned](/reference/provisioned-tools) — no key needed, pay with credits.
- **Already pay for Notion, HubSpot, Instantly, etc.?** Connect them here with your key and keep using your own plan.

Some capabilities exist in both worlds (e.g. web data) — use provisioned when you want zero setup, BYOK when you'd rather use your own account and billing.
