Speed to lead is the whole game: the business that replies first usually wins the deal. Orbit lead forms turn a new signup into an agent reaching out **in seconds**.

## How it works

1. **Build a form** on the CRM page, or point any landing page or Zapier flow at Orbit.
2. A lead lands in the form's [tracker](/concepts/vault) and the [CRM](/concepts/crm), with its phone number.
3. **The form's agent wakes at once** and reaches out by email, text, WhatsApp, or LinkedIn, or drafts the first message for your yes.

You choose, per form, how the agent may reach out, and which agent handles it. **Orbie or any Orbit agent can answer new leads**, or you can make a brand-new agent right in the picker: say in a few sentences what it should do with a new lead and Orbit builds it and assigns it.

## The form designer

Build the form in the app: fields, dropdowns, checkboxes, your colors, font, and corners, with a live preview.

- **Match my website:** Orbit takes your site's brand color, font, and corners automatically.
- **Live embed in two lines** that follows every saved change, plus plain HTML for site builders that strip scripts.
- **No agent, if you prefer:** a form can simply collect leads into its tracker and the CRM.

## Keeping an eye on it

Each form has a log: the latest leads, the run each one woke, and what the agent noted.
