Orbit scales from one person to a team, and from one business to an agency running Orbit for every client.

## Teams

- **Invite teammates by email** and switch between workspaces.
- Everyone shares the same agents, Brain, and dashboards.

## Agency mode

Give **each client its own workspace**, filled in from their website, all drawing on **one credit pool** with an optional limit per client.

- **The Clients screen** shows every client's numbers in one place: credits against their limit, hours worked, tasks, deliverables, what needs a yes, and last activity. Open any client in a click.

## One connection reaches every client

With a single Orbit connection in Claude, ChatGPT, or any AI tool, **name the client and the work lands in that client's workspace**. Leave the name out and it stays in your own. A name that matches no client is refused, so work never lands in the wrong place.

## Send work to a client

**Send to a client** lives right on the thing itself: in a file's or a tracker's **Share**, and in an agent's menu. When you send an agent, it is rewritten for the client with your names, emails, phone numbers, links, and prices taken out. Files and trackers are copied across.
