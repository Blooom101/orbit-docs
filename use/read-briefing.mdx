Your [Briefing](/concepts/briefing) is the first thing to open each morning — one card per agent, summarizing what each did while you were away.

## Steps

1. Open your dashboard. The **Briefing** is your default view.
2. Scan the cards top to bottom — each shows the agent, a one-line summary, and when it ran.
3. Click any card to expand what it filed, and click through to read anything in full.

## What to look for

- **Agents that didn't run** when you expected — a sign something's blocked.
- **Unusually short summaries** — the agent may have hit a problem or had nothing to do.
- **Anything needing your input** — those also land in your [Inbox](/use/process-inbox), where you'll act on them.

Think of the Briefing as the headlines and the [Inbox](/use/process-inbox) as your to-do list.
