The Dashboard is your home screen. It answers the question you actually care about every morning: **what did my agents get done, and what needs me?**

## The four numbers at the top

Across the top you get the headline of your operation, over the last 7 or 30 days:

- **Hours your agents worked**
- **An estimate of the human hours that replaced**
- **Tasks finished**
- **Deliverables** produced (reports, files, and drafts waiting for your yes)

Each number shows the change against the period before, so you can see whether the operation is speeding up.

## Below the headline

- **KPIs** for the things that move a business: people who wrote back, items awaiting your approval, agents that are blocked, and calls booked.
- **Replies to answer**, each with a **Draft with AI** button so you can clear your inbox in a couple of clicks.
- **The CRM pipeline**, so you see where every deal sits without leaving the page.
- **A chart of agent runs** over time.
- **Pinned [Views](/concepts/views)**, so the exact boards and numbers you care about live right here.

## Why it works this way

Running a one-person company with an AI team only works if you can see the whole thing at a glance. The Dashboard rolls up the Briefing, the pipeline, and the work waiting on you into a single screen, so directing your team takes minutes, not an afternoon.
