Orbit has two inboxes, and it helps to keep them straight. The **approvals Inbox** is where your agents' new work waits for you. The **People Inbox** is where messages from real people land.

## The approvals Inbox

Your daily ops surface. It shows every output and report from your agents that is new and has not been reviewed yet.

### Grouped by urgency

- **Needs Action** — conflicts detected, flags raised, items needing your decision
- **Ready to File** — standard agent outputs you can approve in one click
- **Recently Actioned** — in case you want to undo something

### Inbox and Vault are not the same

The Inbox is a filtered view of the Vault's agent outputs. When something arrives from an agent, it shows up as new. Once you review or approve it, it is marked as seen and stays in the [Vault](/concepts/vault) permanently. The Vault also holds everything from your own sessions, the work you did with AI.

### Why it works this way

Agents produce a lot. If everything went straight into permanent memory with no review, your Vault would fill with unverified output.

## The People Inbox

Every reply from a real person, from every channel, in one list: **LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Telegram, X, email, Instantly, and SMS.**

- **A draft is ready.** Orbit suggests a reply from the thread and your ICP, offer, objections, and brand voice. Nothing sends until you click.
- **Replies go out from your own account.**
- **CRM stages are shown** next to each thread, and newsletters are kept out.
