A View is a saved lens over a [tracker](/concepts/vault) or the Vault: a set of filters, a sort, chosen columns, lanes, or a single number. It always reflects the latest data, so you build it once and it stays fresh.

## Make one

- **On Brain > Views,** with a live preview as you set it up.
- **Or just ask in chat:** "show my leads by stage as a board."

## Where a View shows up

- As a **card under the agent's reply** in chat.
- **Pinned to the [Dashboard](/concepts/dashboard)** or a project page.
- As a **read-only share link** with no login, showing only that View's rows, so you can hand a board to someone outside your workspace.

## Why Views matter

Your agents fill trackers with hundreds of rows. A View is how you turn that raw data into the one board or number you actually watch: open deals by stage, replies this week, leads from a campaign. No spreadsheet work, and it never goes out of date.
