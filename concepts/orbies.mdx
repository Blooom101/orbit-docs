An **Orbie** is a ready-to-run agent — a provisioned AI teammate with its integrations already wired in, that you launch in one click. Instead of building an agent from scratch, you pick an Orbie and it's working in minutes.

## The idea

Most "AI agents" are a pile of setup: connect this tool, write that prompt, wire up an integration, hope it holds together. Orbies skip all of that. Each one comes **pre-wired** — the tools and integrations it needs (like [Apify](https://apify.com) for web data, and others) are already connected. You launch it, point it at your goal, and it runs.

Think of an Orbie as hiring someone who already knows the tools and shows up ready to work — not a blank hire you have to train from zero.

## Why this matters for the ICP

If you're building a **one-person company with a full AI team**, Orbies are how you staff it fast:

- **One click to launch** instead of hours of setup.
- **Integrations pre-wired**, so an agent can actually *do* things (pull data, take actions), not just chat.
- **Runs in Orbit Cloud** — the always-on runtime where your agents live, so they work whether your laptop is open or not.
- **Plugs into the same brain** — every Orbie reads your decisions and files its work to your Vault, just like any other agent.

## Orbies + the rest of Orbit

An Orbie isn't a separate world — it's a fast way to add a member to your [org chart](/concepts/org-chart). Once launched, it behaves like any [agent](/concepts/agents): it has an identity, a role, a project, follows your [decisions](/concepts/decisions), and reports into your [Briefing](/concepts/briefing).

So you can mix them freely: some agents you connect from your own tools, some you launch as ready-made Orbies. They all share one memory and one dashboard.

## Orbit Cloud

Orbies run on **Orbit Cloud** — the always-on runtime where your agents live and run. That's what lets a provisioned agent keep working on a schedule without your computer being on. (Your own connected tools can run locally or in the cloud; Orbies are cloud-native by default.)
