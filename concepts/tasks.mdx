Tasks is one list that shows everything in motion across your company: work you gave an agent, work you gave a teammate, tasks from the [Calendar](/concepts/calendar) and from Department Leads, and runs happening right now.

## What it pulls in

- **Tasks you give an agent, a teammate, or yourself.**
- **Tasks from the Calendar** and from Department Leads.
- **Runs going on right now.**

## Requests sit apart

When an agent needs something from you, a question or a draft waiting for your yes, those **requests** sit folded away from your tasks, with a single **Dismiss all**. A request that goes two weeks without an answer lets the agent go ahead without it, so nothing stalls forever.

## Finding your way around

- **A department filter** narrows the list to one team.
- Tasks live in the Calendar as its **Tasks** view, open to everyone on your workspace.
