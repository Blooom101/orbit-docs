The CRM is where everyone your agents talk to, and every deal they work, lives in one place. It is built on your [trackers](/concepts/vault), so the people your agents find and message are already here. Nothing to import to get started.

## The people board

A board of everyone in any tracker, laid out in shared stages: **New, Contacted, Replied, Call booked, Won, Lost.** Drag a card to a new stage and the underlying tracker row updates with it. Agents read the CRM too, so they always know where a person stands.

- **Booked meetings move people to Call booked on their own** (with a connected Gmail or Outlook calendar).
- **Editable stages:** rename, reorder, add, or remove stages for both people and deals.
- **Upload contacts from CSV** with column matching, up to 2,000 at a time.

## The contact card

Open anyone and you get:

- **Overview** with their links and an AI read on them.
- **Conversations** across every channel (LinkedIn, WhatsApp, email, and the rest) plus a timeline.
- **Timeline** of everything that happened: stage changes, notes, messages, meetings, deals, and agent tasks.
- **An owner agent** on every card, so you know who is working the relationship.

## Deals

Track value, currency, close date, owner agent, notes, and a lost reason. The top of the board shows **open pipeline, won in the last 30 days, and win rate.** Win a deal and its contact moves to **Won** automatically.

## Put your agents on it

- **Bulk actions:** tick up to 50 people and ask an agent to reach out, follow up, or research them. Orbit workers start at once.
- Every new lead from a [lead form](/use/lead-forms) or a connected channel lands here with its details.
