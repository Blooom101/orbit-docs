The Calendar is one plan for anything with a date. Posts, agent actions, events, tasks, reminders, and deadlines all sit on the same week, for you and your agents.

## What is on it

- **Everything with a date,** in one view, color-coded by type.
- **Your real meetings,** pulled in read-only from a connected Google or Outlook calendar, or any calendar link. Clashes are marked.
- **Click a day** and a side panel opens with a card for each thing happening.
- **A department filter,** plus a **Today** card on the [Dashboard](/concepts/dashboard).

## Agents plan on it too

The Calendar is not just a picture of the plan, it runs it:

- **Put an action on a date and Orbit starts the agent at that time.** Scheduled social posts show up on their own.
- **Agents in any AI client plan here.** Ask Claude to "plan next week's posts" and they appear on the Orbit Calendar. Agents check your meetings before booking, and if you move or cancel an agent's item, the agent makes the outside world match.

## Tasks

The **Tasks** view lives inside the Calendar: one list of who is working on what, agents and people together. See [Tasks](/concepts/tasks).

## Goals and campaigns

Set a **goal** with a reach-by date and key results you tick off, or lay a **campaign** across its run of days. Every agent run is told which goals and campaigns are live, so the whole team plans toward the same thing.

## Operations view

The **Operations** view shows every scheduled agent by day, grouped by where it runs, with Orbit workers first and busy days folded. It is the control tower for a company that runs on schedules.
