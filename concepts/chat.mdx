Chat is the front door to Orbit — and the primary way to create almost anything. You describe an **outcome**, and **Orbie** (your Orbit assistant) figures out the rest: it designs the agents, picks the right tools, and files the work where it belongs. It's the closest thing to hiring — without hiring.

## Describe the outcome, not the steps

You don't configure agents field by field. You say what you want:

> "Find companies matching my ICP every morning, enrich the decision-makers, and drop them into a tracker."

> "Watch my competitors' sites and pricing weekly and write me a short brief on what changed."

> "Read what's in my Vault each week and draft posts in my voice, ready for me to review."

Orbie thinks about the rest — which agents to create, which tools they need, how they should run — and sets it up. You review and approve.

## It replaces hiring

Instead of hiring for a role, you describe the role and Orbie builds the agent that fills it:

- **GTM engineer** — lead sourcing, enrichment, signal watching, wired into your tools.
- **GTM / growth marketer** — research → content → outreach, on a schedule.
- **Social media marketer** — drafts and posts in your voice across channels.
- **Automations** — several agents chained into one workflow (see below).

One person, describing outcomes, ends up running the output of a whole team.

## It can build automations, not just single agents

The revolutionary part: Orbie doesn't only make one agent — it can wire **several agents into an [automation](/concepts/automations)** where each works on the previous one's output, in sequence. A research agent feeds a writer, which feeds a publisher. You describe the end-to-end outcome; Orbie builds the chain.

This is the cross-coordination that's been missing from AI and agent development: agents that hand off to each other, all sharing one memory, all governed by your decisions.

## It also answers anything about your setup

Chat isn't only for creating. Ask it about your own data and workforce:

- "What did my agents produce this week?"
- "Which leads did we contact and what happened?"
- "What's in my Vault about pricing?"

Because Orbie sits on top of your Vault, decisions, and every agent's output, it can answer from *your* company's real data — not generic knowledge.

## What you'll see

- A box that says **"Describe what you want to create."**
- Chips to scope it: **Project**, **Department**, **Add context**, a **model** picker, and **History**.
- Suggested starting points like *Build agents to grow my business*, *Help me set Orbit up*, *Watch my competitors*, *Turn research into content*, *Find me new leads*.

## Chat vs. the other create paths

| Path | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Chat (Orbie)** | Describe an outcome; Orbie builds agents or a whole automation. The default. |
| **[Launch an Orbie](/use/launch-orbie)** | Grab a ready-made agent in one click. |
| **[Agent Builder](/use/agent-builder)** | Hands-on build of one agent from a goal. |

All three land the same place: real teammates in your AI company that share your memory and report to you.
