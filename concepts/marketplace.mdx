The Marketplace is where you grab a working teammate without building one. It has a **Verified by Orbit** section: agents Orbit has tested, each added with a single click and set to start on your own site or profile.

## What is in there

The verified set includes agents for:

- **LinkedIn growth** and **lead finding**
- **Email outreach** and **meeting prep**
- **Investor outreach**, **Job Finder**, and **Upwork**
- **X ghostwriter**, **Social Content Creator**, and **Reddit**
- **Inbox** (reply drafting) and **directory submission**

## How it works

Add one and it starts from your website or profile, so its first run is already about your business. From there it is a normal agent: it shares your [Brain](/concepts/knowledge), obeys your [decisions](/concepts/decisions), and reports to you.
