Agents in Orbit do not start from scratch every run. They record what worked and how you want things done, and they review their own recent work on a regular beat, so the operation gets sharper over time instead of resetting.

## How the learning happens

- **Agents record learnings** as they work: what succeeded, and the preferences you corrected them on.
- **Every 10 runs or 7 days**, an agent reviews its recent work and pulls out what to keep doing.
- **Learnings have a scope:** kept to one agent, shared with a project, a department, or the whole workspace. A lesson one agent learns can lift the whole team.

## Agents can plan their own schedule

Ask in chat and an agent will schedule itself ("I will run every Monday at 9") or set up another agent to cover a gap. New agents are only ever created with your yes.

## Why this matters

This is the difference between a tool and a teammate. A tool does the same thing every time. An Orbit agent remembers the win, the correction, and the preference, and applies it next time, so your company's know-how compounds in the [Brain](/concepts/knowledge) rather than living in your head.
