Orbit is a subscription plus credits. Your plan includes a number of agents and a pool of **credits** (credits pay for runs, model calls, and tool calls). You can top up with pay-as-you-go credits any time.

## The plans

- **Free** — try Orbit and run your first agents.
- **Pro** — for a solo operator running a full AI team. Runs on Claude Sonnet 5.5 in Pro power mode.
- **Agency** — a separate workspace per client, one shared credit pool, and the Clients screen. See [Teams and agencies](/use/teams-agencies).
- **GTM Partnership** — a hands-on lane for go-to-market.

<Note>
  For exact prices, credit allowances, and current limits, the live [billing page](https://app.orbitagents.com) is always the source of truth.
</Note>

## What uses credits

- **Agent runs** and the model behind them. Power modes: Normal (MiniMax M3 or Claude Haiku 5.5), Pro (Claude Sonnet 5.5), and Max.
- **Provisioned tools** (web data, scraping, images, video, voice), billed per use. See [Provisioned tools](/reference/provisioned-tools).
- **Browser time** for agents that use a real browser.

## Add-ons

- **Connected accounts** (LinkedIn, Gmail, and the rest) are a small monthly add-on per account. See [Connect your accounts](/use/connect-your-accounts).
- **Refer & earn:** get credits for things like a backlink, a survey, or a testimonial.
